# Design System: ChaosRoad (ElevenLabs Editorial System)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Quietly editorial print magazine meets modern mobility platform.
- **Canvas:** Off-white `#f5f5f5` (`--canvas`) and `#fafafa` (`--canvas-soft`)
- **Surfaces:** Pure white `#ffffff` (`--surface-card`) with `1px solid #e7e5e4` (`--hairline`) outlines and subtle `0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)` hover elevation
- **Ink & Typography:** Warm near-black `#0c0a09` (`--ink`) and `#292524` (`--primary`), body `#4e4e4e`, muted `#777169`
- **Atmospheric Orbs:** Soft radial blooms in Mint (`#a7e5d3`), Peach (`#f4c5a8`), Lavender (`#c8b8e0`), Sky (`#a8c8e8`), and Rose (`#e8b8c4`) used strictly as background atmosphere
- **Geometry:** Pill buttons and badges (`9999px`), `16px` (`--rounded-xl`) cards, `24px` (`--rounded-xxl`) hero/orb containers, `8px` (`--rounded-md`) inputs
